What you're looking at

The registration flow, with a spine

The live registration is four screens long and tells you nothing about that. There is no stepper, no "2 of 4", no back-context — you answer questions until they stop. Every frame here carries the same progress component under the back arrow, so the flow has a beginning, a middle and an end. Frame 4 shows that component at all five positions.

The pathway screen is the product's only pricing and tier surface, and the live build renders both tiers in the same box, at the same size, with the same button. It gives no steer at all. Here the member card is the hero — lighter fill, an accent ring, a bigger title, a solid accent button — and the guest card recedes into the dark section fill with an outline button. The PREMIUM badge has moved out of the middle of a bullet list, where it appeared to qualify one benefit, into the card header where it qualifies the tier.

Two pieces of copy in the live build are doing real work and were nearly invisible: we check your ID — the legal backbone of a product that signs children up through adults — and use an avatar, not a real photo, which is genuinely good privacy thinking. Both were set in the screen's lowest text emphasis. Both are now framed notices with an accent icon, a white headline and body copy, at the top of the group they govern.

The product name appeared five times in the thirteen strings on the live pathway screen, including Browse Sports Heist app. It appears once here, in the line under the title, spelled SportsHeist.

  • Icon button — the 38px circle carries the back arrow
  • Status pill — accent wash with a leading dot, reused as the step counter
  • Section container — #080A0C with a hairline, used for the progress sheet in frame 4
  • Row construction — hairline-separated rows for the benefit lists
  • Avatar — circle, cover fill, focal point center 22%

New in this proposal

  • Stepper — four labelled segments with a part-filled current bar, plus a step-count pill. Nothing like it exists in the live flow
  • Four-step model — Pathway → Details → Identity → Clubs, read off the captured exits
  • Tier hierarchy — hero fill, accent ring, larger title and a solid button on the member card against a recessed guest card
  • Tier badge in the header — PREMIUM moved off the benefit row; LIMITED added as its guest counterpart
  • Matched icon pair — one medal silhouette drawn twice, star and no star, replacing a flat grey person glyph opposite a gold rosette
  • Exclusion row — what the guest tier cannot do, stated on the card
  • Primary button — 52px, 12px radius, uppercase 13px/700/.08em; the pattern table has no CTA, so this is a proposal
  • Notice card — the frame for the ID-check and privacy messages that were previously muted body copy
  • Aligned two-up row — grid with a fixed label row, fixing the ~8pt misalignment in both live pairs
  • Geography chain — country drives province and dial code, so the form can no longer hold a Vietnamese dial code, a Vietnamese address and an Italian country at once
  • Date-of-birth format hint and age echo — resolves 02/06/2001 being readable two ways
  • Divided phone control — a fill change and hairline between the dial code and the number
  • Username states — checking, available, taken, plus suggestion pills and a disabled CTA with its reason
  • Mark as a picture option — the brand peaks as a non-photographic choice, shown selected
  • Focus treatment — accent border and halo on the focused control
  • Copy — every string on frames 1–3 rewritten; the product name used once instead of five times, and Browse Sports Heist app retired
  • In-flow primary buttons — review scaffolding only. Each screen already stands alone on its own device; in build the primary button is pinned above the safe area

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. Is membership required, or is guest access real?

    The screen before this one states that membership is mandatory. This one offers a guest pathway with its own benefit list and its own button. Both cannot be true, and the contradiction is load-bearing: it decides whether the pathway screen is a fork or a soft sell. We have built it as a real fork with a strong steer toward membership — guest access exists and is genuinely usable. If membership is actually mandatory, the guest card should become a "look around first" link and the sign-up copy needs rewriting.

  2. What does membership cost?

    PREMIUM appears in the live build with no price, no comparison and no inclusion list, on the product's only tier screen. We have kept the badge and added no price anywhere, and labelled the other tier LIMITED. If there is a fee, a subscription or a club-paid arrangement, this screen is where it belongs and the cards need a price line.

  3. Can a guest become a member later?

    We tell them so, under the cards, and frame 4 assumes they rejoin the flow at step 2 of 4 with the pathway step already complete. If there is no upgrade path, that line and that behaviour both come out.

  4. Is registration four steps?

    The stepper is Pathway, Details, Identity, Clubs, taken from the captured entry and exit points. Email and password are collected somewhere too — if that is its own screen the count becomes five, and if club selection can be skipped it may not deserve a segment. Confirm the real sequence before build; the labels are the flow's public promise.

  5. Is the legal name ever shown to other members?

    Frame 3 says the details from step 2 are never shown, which is what makes the username and avatar advice coherent. We have assumed legal name and address are never public. If a club sees a member's real name, the copy has to say so instead.

  6. What are the username rules?

    Shown as 3–20 characters, letters, numbers and underscores and three generated suggestions. Both are placeholders for whatever the API enforces — we need the real constraint, the reserved-word list, and whether suggestions come from the server.

  7. Which field owns geography?

    We made country the master: it filters province and sets the dial code, and the dial code can still be changed by hand with a warning line. Confirm that a member can hold a phone number from a country other than their residence — there are legitimate reasons for that, and the alternative is to hard-lock it.

  8. What happens on the under-18 path?

    It is stated twice in the live build and branches nowhere. We have kept it as a note on the member card and in the ID notice. Before build we need the actual mechanism — a guardian's email, a code from an existing member, a blocked state — because a note that leads nowhere is the worst version of it.

  9. Gender options?

    The repository offers two. We show Male as a filled example and have not invented a list. Confirm the options, whether the field is required, and whether it exists for ID matching or for something else.
