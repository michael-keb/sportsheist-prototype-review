Don’t Allow Allow

Drawn at iOS's real geometry — 270 pt wide, R14, title 17/600, message 13/400, 44 pt buttons split by hairlines — and rendered greyscale on purpose. iOS paints those two labels in system blue #007AFF, a hue that appears nowhere in this brand and that the app cannot override; greying it keeps a colour we do not own out of the library while still showing exactly what is inherited.

The shipped build renders this title as “Sports Heist(stg)” Would Like to Send You Notifications — the staging flavour suffix leaking through CFBundleDisplayName. It is a build setting in ios/Runner/Info.plist and the Android manifest label, not a design value, and it must read SportsHeist before release.
