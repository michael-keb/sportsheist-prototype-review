The card a member sees for every idea in the War Room. It carries who proposed it, what it is, how long is left, and how close it is to the support it needs to become a Proposal — then gives the member the one action that moves it forward.

What you're looking at

The most-repeated component in the product

Ideas are the entry point to everything the War Room does. A member posts one, other members like it, and if it clears its like threshold before the clock runs out it becomes a Proposal and goes to a vote. This card is how that whole mechanic is expressed, and it appears in a horizontal rail on the War Room home, in the Ideas list, and again inside search results.

Because it recurs so often, its geometry sets the standard for every other card in the system — the voting card, the result card and the action card are all the same shell with the middle panel swapped. Every value on it was sampled from the Figma export rather than approximated: the card measures 268 px wide with 16 px padding at R16, and the panel and pill inside it are R12 and fully rounded respectively.

Provenance

What is reused and what is new

Reused from the Figma file

  • The whole card — this component is drawn in the War Room frames; the build reproduces it
  • Card geometry — 268 px, 16 px padding, R16, measured off the export
  • Both action states — the accent Like and dark Liked pills are both in the frames
  • Gauge — ring size, stroke weight, round caps and clockwise-from-top direction
  • Type tag and countdown — including the leading dot and the stopwatch glyph
  • Section header — accent +, filter button and View all ↗ pill
  • Photography — the author portraits are lifted from the same exports

New in this proposal

  • Gauge draw-in — the ring animates on scroll-into-view; the Figma frames are static
  • Like ⇄ Liked as a live toggle — the frames show the two states separately, not the transition
  • Ticking countdown — a real clock rather than a fixed string
  • Avatar stacking rule — overlap and ring weight for multi-author ideas, inferred from a two-avatar frame
  • Focus states — visible keyboard focus on the action, absent from the frames

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. What happens to an idea when the clock hits zero?

    One Figma frame shows a card at 00:00:00 that is still in its Liked state, which reads as a live card with a dead timer. An expired idea needs its own treatment — lapsed, promoted, or held — and none exists yet.

  2. Can a member un-like an idea?

    The card is built so the action toggles both ways, because that is the honest reading of two states on one control. If likes are final once cast, the Liked state should be inert and styled as a confirmation rather than a button.

  3. Is the gauge a percentage of a threshold, or of a target?

    The label reads 75% of required likes, so the ring fills toward a bar rather than a total. If the threshold can move as membership changes, the card should show the count as well as the proportion.

  4. Title case for card titles?

    Titles are set sentence case here, matching the Figma. The live build generates some titles automatically as Auto Position Proposal <epoch-ms>, which will look broken in this card at any casing — worth fixing at the source.
