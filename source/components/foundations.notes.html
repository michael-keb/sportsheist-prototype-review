Not a phone screen. This is the reference sheet for the whole rebrand — every surface, every hex, the type scale at true size, the measured spacing, all fourteen controls with all their states, and the icon set. Two badges run through it: Sampled means the value already exists in the export, Proposed means we made it and it needs your sign-off.

What you're looking at

The single source of truth, and its edges

Everything else in this library is a screen. This is the sheet those screens are built from. It is exempt from the 375 px target — it runs to 1180 px wide with 24 px side padding, because side-by-side comparison is the point of it — but it obeys every other rule: sampled tokens only, the system font stack, inline SVG icons, no external requests.

Two things make it more than a swatch page. First, the contrast figures are computed from the hex values with the WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula rather than eyeballed, and they are stated to two decimal places — so where a pairing fails, the sheet says so plainly, including in places the export already ships. Second, the badges. Six of the fourteen controls come from the export; the other eight were built here from the established language and do not exist yet. The sheet does not blur that line, and neither should the review.

The short version of what it establishes: four near-black surfaces, one accent, one semantic red, and nothing else. Every distinction in the product comes from scale, weight, ring opacity or the accent wash — never from a new hue.

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. Eight of the fourteen controls do not exist yet — sign them off?

    Sampled and shippable today: filled accent pill, dark pill, View all ↗ pill, circular icon button, status pill, type tag. Proposed and needing a decision: outlined accent pill, segmented control, text field, select, search field, toggle, checkbox, radio. Every form, sheet and settings screen in the library depends on that second list, so it is the gating item. Each one was derived from a sampled pattern rather than invented — the field shell is the sunken panel, the segmented control is the tab-bar rule — but derived is not approved.

  2. Are the touch targets acceptable?

    34 px and 38 px are both under the 44 px minimum. In the export they sit in enough padding that the tappable area clears it, but nothing enforces that, and a control moved into a tighter layout will quietly fail. We chose 44 px for the proposed field height so at least one control in the system satisfies the target on its own. The decision is whether to raise the existing two, or to add a written padding rule that keeps them legal.

  3. Red on a card misses AA by 0.14 — do we change it or ring-fence it?

    #E2564A on #1C2129 computes to 4.36:1 against a 4.5 requirement. The one place it actually lands there today — the leaderboard delta — is 11/600 with a heavy arrow beside it and the number duplicated by the rank column, so it reads. But it is the weakest pair in the system. Options: lighten the red slightly for use on --surface, keep it and forbid it below a stated size and weight, or move it to --sunken where it clears at 4.84. This build has taken the second option; it needs to be a rule, not a habit.

  4. Should the icon-weight range in the contract be rewritten?

    The contract says 1.7–1.9. The export actually runs 1.5 to 3.2, and the pattern underneath is optical rather than fixed — the smaller the render, the heavier the stroke, so a glyph holds its weight at 10 px. The five proposed glyphs follow that same rule. We suggest the contract should read 1.5–3.2, scaled optically instead.

  5. Is --dim allowed to stay where it is?

    It fails AA for body text on every surface — 2.56:1 on --surface, 3.26:1 at best on --bg. In the export it only ever carries the tracked micro-label, which is decorative and never the sole route to information, and this build keeps it strictly there. Confirm that constraint, because the temptation to use it for secondary body copy will come up on every dense screen.

  6. Three summary counts on the sheet do not match what it renders

    The masthead claims twelve type steps and sixteen glyphs; the footer claims eleven sampled glyphs. What is actually on the page is fourteen type specimens across thirteen distinct sizes and eighteen glyphs, thirteen sampled and five proposed. The specimens and the badges are correct — only the prose totals are stale. Tell us which numbers you want quoted externally and we will make the counts and the copy agree.

  7. Does this sheet become the contract?

    It currently documents the system; the build contract is a separate markdown file, and the two already disagree on icon weight. One of them has to be authoritative. Our recommendation is that this page becomes the reference and the contract shrinks to the rules that cannot be shown — structure, asset handling, naming — with everything measurable living here.
