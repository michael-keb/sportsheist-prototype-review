The rail card a member sees while a proposal is open for voting, and the ballot they open to cast a ranked vote. It is the Idea card's sibling: the same shell, with the likes gauge replaced by a threshold bar.

What you're looking at

One shell, two data readings

The War Room rails carry two card types. The Idea card counts likes towards a threshold and draws them as a ring. The Voting card counts the vote and draws it as a bar, because a vote has something a like does not: a line it has to cross. A ring cannot show a line; a bar can.

Everything else is identical — 268 px wide, 16 px padding, R16, author row, title, body, countdown and type, panel, then one action. Put the two side by side in the rail and they read as one family.

The bar states the whole position in one glance: how far the vote has got, where it has to get to, and whether it is going to make it. The verdict is written out in words as well, so the card does not depend on colour alone.

Provenance

What is reused and what is new

Reused from the Figma file

  • Card shell — 268 px, 16 px padding, R16, author row, title, body, countdown and type row, panel, action. Taken from the Idea card.
  • Threshold bar — track, accent fill, marker knob and threshold tick, as drawn in the dark-mode export.
  • Verdict pill — ON TRACK TO BE APPROVED in accent on --acid-wash, tracked uppercase at 10 px / 700.
  • Panel lede and leading option — Option 1 leading at 13.5 px / 600 over the option name at 15 px / 600, with the circled ! at the right.
  • Outlined CTA — transparent pill, 1 px accent border, accent label with a leading glyph.
  • Highlighted row — accent wash plus a 3 px inset accent rule, the treatment the Merits leaderboard uses for the member's own row.
  • Ballot content — the option set and the Cancel / Vote pair, from the current build's voting detail.

New in this proposal

  • Visible rank slots — the current build tells members to click options until they are numbered, then renders no number, badge, handle or checkbox. The slot is drawn empty and fills with the rank, so the instruction and the screen finally agree.
  • Ranked progress pill — Ranked 0/4, so a member can see they have not finished before they reach for a disabled button.
  • Short instruction — one line inside the panel replaces the build's four-line 118 pt navy banner. The banner also carried the only blue in the War Room.
  • Veto-hold treatment — the build has frozen-bar tokens but no recorded design. Every moving part greys; only the target stays accent.
  • Two derived washes — rgba(226,86,74,.14) for the off-track pill and track, and rgba(255,255,255,.07) for the frozen pill. Both are built the way --acid-wash is built from --acid: no new hue, only a transparency of a colour already in the set.
  • No per-option colour — the build gives each option its own hue as a bright avatar and a dark swatch. The rebrand has one accent, so rank and share carry the distinction instead.

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. Is the verdict a prediction or a statement of fact?

    ON TRACK TO BE APPROVED reads as a forecast. If the vote is counted by elimination rounds, a leading option on 74% of first preferences is not yet approved and the wording may promise more than the count can deliver. We have assumed the pill states the current standing against the threshold, not a prediction of the final round. Confirm, or give us the wording you want.

  2. Should the closed card say what was decided?

    The closed state currently reads Option 2 approved plus RESULT PUBLISHED, and its CTA reads View result rather than View details. Confirm that a settled proposal keeps its place in the Voting rail at all, rather than moving to Results.

  3. Can a member vote without ranking every option?

    The build's instruction says to rank until all are numbered, so we disable Vote until the ballot is complete. If a partial ranking is a valid vote, the button should enable on the first rank and we need to know what happens to the options left blank.

  4. What does the circled ! open?

    We have it opening an explanation of how the threshold works. The build uses a circled ? beside the taxonomy labels for something similar. One glyph and one behaviour across the app would be better than two.

  5. Does a veto stop the clock or the count?

    We freeze both: the countdown greys and the bar stops. If a veto only blocks the outcome while voting continues, the bar should stay live and only the verdict pill should change.
