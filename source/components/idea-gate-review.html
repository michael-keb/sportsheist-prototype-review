Idea gate

The screen a member meets on the way to creating an Idea — the one that says whether they are allowed to and what it costs. It has a route (CRoute.warningIdea, referenced 7 times and pushed from five) and it has never been captured or rebuilt. The live version gates on nothing.

As built
warning_create.dart is a stateless screen: a raster lamp, the Bebas line SUBMIT YOUR IDEA!, one centred paragraph, and a CONTINUE button that always pushes the form. No permission read, no balance read, no cost figure, no branch.
The gap
The paragraph explains Likes, Proposals and lapsing accurately — and never mentions that posting costs 25.0 Credits, which is the only thing on this screen a member could get wrong at a price.
Now gates on
Three signals the app already fetches: the club's per-category posting permissions, the member's available Credits against the deposit, and whether the fixture will outlast the Idea's own stages.
Currency
Credits, one noun, always with a figure. A deposit of 25.0 Credits, returned when the Idea becomes a Proposal, forfeited only if it lapses.

What happens next

    You post it

    Your Idea goes into the War Room and the Ideas stage opens for 6 minutes.

    25.0 Credits are locked

  2. 2

    It collects Likes

    Reach 120 Likes before the 6 minutes end and it becomes a Proposal. The club then votes for 3 minutes.

    25.0 Credits returned in full

  3. 3

    Or it lapses

    Short of 120 when the clock ends, the Idea closes and never reaches a vote. This is the only way the deposit does not come back.

    25.0 Credits forfeited

  • Nothing is locked yet. The deposit is taken when you post, not when you open the form — you can write an Idea and discard it for free.

  • If the match ends while your Idea is still running, it is cancelled automatically and the deposit is returned.

What Barcelona lets you post

  • Positions Who plays, who captains, who is rested. Open
  • On-field decisions Instructions the coach can carry out during the match. Open

Set by the club, per category. Off-field and management decisions are never member Ideas at any club, so they are not listed — a category a member can never use is not a locked door, it is somebody else's building.

You'll see the deposit again on the last step, with a figure and a confirmation, before anything is locked.

The verb is repeated, per the dialog convention: “Start an idea” → Start an idea. It is deliberately not “Post idea” — that verb belongs to the last step of the form, where the deposit is actually taken. The live screen's affirmative is CONTINUE, shouted, which names neither the act nor its cost. The secondary is Not now rather than Cancel, because the ask can come back.

Start this late?

Barcelona v Girona has about 7 minutes left. An Idea needs 6 minutes of Likes and 3 minutes of voting, so the match will almost certainly finish first.

  • If it does, your Idea is cancelled automatically and your 25.0 Credits come straight back. You lose the Idea, not the deposit.

  • A Position Idea keeps running after the whistle. Only on-field decisions have to land inside the match.

A caution, not a block — the member is eligible and the money is safe, so the affirmative stays acid and stays on the right. This is the one state that earns the route's name: warningIdea currently warns about nothing. Copy comes from the two strings the build already ships — ideaPage.this game still run and the cancellation lines in result/result_count.dart — which are the only places the app admits the match clock can outrun an Idea. Warning is red on the neutral wash with no ring, one step below error, exactly as in the dialogs kit.

03Category closed

What Barcelona lets you post

  • PositionsWho plays, who captains, who is rested. Open
  • On-field decisionsInstructions the coach can carry out during the match. Club only

Post a Position idea instead?

Barcelona has on-field decisions switched off for member Ideas. Only the club can put those forward, and that is the club's setting, not anything about your account.

  • Positions is open to you — same 6-minute Ideas stage, same 3-minute vote, same deposit of 25.0 Credits.

  • Nothing has been locked and no Credits have moved.

The real gate. ConfigClub.permissions already carries member_post_idea_positions and member_post_idea_on_field_decision per club, and the app reads them in exactly one place — ideas/drop_down/title.dart — to decide how many category tiles the compose form draws. That logic has a hole: findList() returns 0 when both permissions are false, and index 0 is rendered anyway, so a member with no posting permission at all is still shown Positions and can still submit. Checking at the gate closes it, and turns a dead end into a question with a way out. A padlock on the neutral tile, not red: this is a rule, not a failure, and nothing has gone wrong or cost anything.

05Suppressed

Create idea

Step 1 of 3 · the form, opened directly

Deposit 25.0 Credits · 915.0 left Locked when you post, returned when your Idea becomes a Proposal.

Once the member has ticked Don't show this again, the gate stops appearing and the form carries this instead — a single line at the top of step 1, with the figure, the balance after it, and a route back to the full explanation. The cost never disappears; only the full-screen interruption does.

Why the gate should be suppressible at all: the route is pushed from five places and shows the same paragraph every single time, so a member who posts twice a week reads it a hundred times a year. A gate that cannot be dismissed is not read — it is tapped through, which is exactly how a member ends up surprised by a forfeited deposit. The disclosure that matters survives as the banner and as the confirm dialog on the last step of the form, both of which carry the figure.

What you're looking at

A gate that currently gates nothing

The route is called warningIdea. What it actually does is show a lamp illustration, the shouted line SUBMIT YOUR IDEA!, one centred paragraph, and a CONTINUE button whose entire body is a push to the create-idea form. There is no permission check, no balance check, no match check, no cost figure and no branch of any kind. warningIdea appears seven times in lib/ — three of those are its own route declaration; the other five are unconditional pushes from an app-bar action on the Ideas, Voting, Results and War Room screens. A full-screen interruption that always says the same thing and always continues.

The paragraph it shows is not wrong. It says an Idea becomes a Proposal with enough Likes in time, otherwise it lapses, and that members then vote to Support or Reject. What it never mentions is that posting locks 25.0 Credits, and lapsing forfeits them — which is the only thing on this screen a member can get wrong at a price.

So this rebuild does two things. It turns the paragraph into a three-step account of what happens to the Idea and to the money, with the figure on screen. And it gates on the three signals the app already fetches before this screen is even reachable: the club's per-category posting permission, the member's available Credits against the deposit, and whether the fixture will outlast the Idea's own stages. Frames 02, 03 and 04 are those three branches; frame 05 is what the member sees once they have asked not to be shown the gate again.

  • Inline info banner — dialogs.html
  • Frame, app bar, spec rows, amount lockup, consent row — create-idea.html, so the gate and the form read as one flow
  • Club crest plate — the rgba(255,255,255,.94) fixed plate from club-selection.html
  • Credits as the one noun, and 25.0 as the deposit — wallet.html and create-idea.html
  • 6-minute Ideas stage, 3-minute vote, 120 Likes — the same figures create-idea.html uses
  • The lifecycle itself — a rewrite of the shipped ideaPage.info create string, which is the live screen's entire content
  • The cancellation-and-refund rule — the two strings in result/result_count.dart
  • The late-post concern — ideaPage.this game still run, which the build already ships
  • The permission model — ConfigClub.permissions.member_post_idea_positions and member_post_idea_on_field_decision, real per-club server fields
  • The deposit figure's source — ConfigClub.creditPostIdea, a real server field

New in this proposal

  • Any gating at all. The live screen reads no permission, no balance, no match state, and has no branch. All four of frames 02–05 are new.
  • The cost on screen. The live gate never mentions Credits. The amount lockup, the balance rows, the per-step money lines and the "nothing is locked yet" row are all new.
  • The permission check. The two permission fields are read in exactly one place today — ideas/drop_down/title.dart — to size the category list on the form. Reading them at the gate is new, and it closes a live hole: findList() returns 0 when both permissions are false and index 0 is then rendered anyway, so a member with no posting permission at all is still offered Positions and can still submit.
  • The balance check. Derivable from creditPostIdea and the wallet today, checked nowhere. As shipped, the first time a member learns they cannot afford to post is after writing the Idea, at the confirm dialog.
  • The match-window check. The app tells a member their Idea was cancelled for running past the whistle; nothing warns them beforehand.
  • The three-step rail — new; the live screen is one centred paragraph
  • The eligibility panel — new; a member cannot see what their club allows anywhere in the product
  • Don't show this again, and the banner that replaces the gate — new
  • Drawn lamp glyph in a 72 px tile — new, replacing two rasters authored for a white scaffold. 72px is a new tile size and only this screen uses it.
  • Verb pair. Start an idea / Not now, replacing CONTINUE — which names neither the act nor its cost, and is shouted.

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. Is 25.0 Credits the deposit?

    25.0 is carried over from wallet.html and create-idea.html, whose ledger arithmetic derives from it. The figure is invented — no pricing exists anywhere in the repository; the server holds it in credit_post_idea and the app renders whatever arrives. The number needs a product answer, and if it is per-club or per-category the gate has to say which, because the panel currently states one figure for both categories.

  2. Which balance is right — 940.0 or 340?

    wallet.html shows 1,240.0 total = 940.0 available + 300.0 locked and this gate follows it. create-idea.html's confirm dialog shows 340 → 315 for the same act. Two components describing the same member's money with different figures will be the first thing anyone notices; one of them has to move before build.

  3. Should the gate really check the balance up front?

    We think yes — telling somebody they can't afford it before they write 200 words is plainly kinder than after. But it changes the shape of the flow: the gate becomes a screen that can refuse, which means it needs the wallet's balance loaded before it paints, and a decision about what happens if the balance changes between the gate and the post. We have assumed the confirm dialog on the last step re-checks and is the authority.

  4. What happens when a club closes both member categories?

    Frame 03 offers Positions as the way out of a closed On-field decisions. If a club switches both off, there is no alternative to offer and the gate becomes a dead end. We think the right answer is to remove the entry point from the app bar entirely rather than show a screen that only says no — but that is five call sites to change, and it needs confirming.

  5. Can the gate be dismissed permanently?

    We have proposed yes, with the cost surviving as a banner on the form and as the existing confirm dialog. The reasoning is that a screen pushed from five places, showing the same paragraph every time, is not read — it is tapped through, which is exactly how somebody ends up surprised by a forfeited deposit. If legal or compliance need the full disclosure every time, say so and the checkbox comes out.

  6. Do you want the illustrated lamp back?

    The live screen composites assets/images/ideas/stack_create.png (a 164pt photograph) with assets/svgs/idea/idea_light.svg (129 × 170), both authored against a white scaffold. Neither has a dark-mode export, so this file uses a drawn 24-box lamp in a 72 px acid-wash tile. If the illustration is wanted, it needs re-cutting for #030401 — we would rather ask than draw a substitute.

  7. Are the category names right for members?

    Positions and On-field decisions are the code's names. The one-line glosses under them ("Who plays, who captains, who is rested" / "Instructions the coach can carry out during the match") are ours, written from CLString's longer definitions. They are the first plain explanation of the categories anywhere in the product and they should be signed off as copy, not inherited from a mock-up.

  8. What counts as "too late" in a match?

    Frame 02 fires when the remaining match time is under the Idea's own stage total — 6 + 3 = 9 minutes at Barcelona, so the caution appears inside the last nine minutes plus stoppage. That rule is ours. Whether stoppage time is known to the client, and whether the threshold should carry a margin, are open.
