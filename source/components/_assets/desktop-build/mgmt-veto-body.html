Club Management’s rationale

The commitment. The two Japan fixtures are contracted with the tournament organiser until July 2027 — not a decision the club can reopen this season.

The cost of withdrawing. Cancelling now triggers a €4.2m penalty and leaves the club in breach of the broadcast agreement covering both matches.

What we are doing instead. A shortened three-day tour, with one fixture instead of two, comes to the club as a new Idea within 48 hours.
