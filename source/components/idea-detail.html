Idea detail

Where a member reads an Idea in full and decides whether to back it. Ten screens: the idea, the progress chart, the three Like states, the final minutes, the author's view, the discussion, a lapse, and the two failures. Rebuilt from lib/pages/ideas/like_count.dart (1,377 lines), which has never been captured beyond one default-state screenshot.

Chart source
Vote history, not the proposal. getListVoteHistory returns one row per counted Like with a timestamp. Y is percent of the required Likes, X is time left in the window, counting down left to right.
Threshold
Per club. thresholds.target_percentage_like × total_member. Here 5% of 1,284 members = 64 Likes.
Currency
One noun: Credits. Deposit 25.0 Credits — returned when the Idea becomes a Proposal, forfeited only on a lapse.
Not pinned
Nothing in this file is fixed to the bottom of the viewport, because nothing is in the live screen either — the Like CTA and the composer are both inside the scroll view, and the review page replays pinned chrome into every frame, which would put a Like button on the lapsed screen.
01The idea

Everything a member needs before the decision, in one screen: who raised it, what it is, how long is left, and how close it is. The IDEA kicker the live build prints above the title is dropped — it repeats both the app-bar title and the active stage.

10Couldn't load

This screen makes three calls in parallel — proposal, vote history, comments — and requires all three to return 200 before it renders anything. Any one failing leaves the member on a shimmer, or, in the catch branch, gets them UDialog().showError(text: e.toString()) — a raw exception string in a dialog. This is the replacement: what failed, what still works, one thing to do.
