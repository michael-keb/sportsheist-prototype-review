The club-management screens reached from Profile: the clubs you are registered with, adding one, the guest-or-member fork, the annual fee and its terms, the two dialogs either side of payment, and the sheet that switches your current club. Twelve screens — eleven of them a proposal, and one, frame 12, the screen that is actually in the app today.

What you're looking at

What ships, and what is a proposal

Read frame 12 first. Frame 12 — Add a club · as shipped — is the screen a member reaches today when they try to join a club. It shows the crest, one line of copy, and a button. The copy is "To add this club, kindly log in to sports heist on desktop", and the button's entire job is to close the sheet. There is no next step. Frames 04 to 10 — the four-step picker, the guest fork, the fee screen, the membership-ID dialog, the receipt and the declined card — build the in-app journey the specs describe, and none of it exists in code. We checked: the one endpoint that would add a club is never called from anywhere in the app, and there is no payment library, no charge call and no card-capture screen in the codebase at all. Frames 04 to 10 are a proposal for work that has not been built, not a rebrand of something that has.

That contrast is the reason frame 12 is in here. A client tapping through frames 04 to 10 would reasonably conclude that joining a club works in the app. It does not, and the review should not imply otherwise.

The rest is the club flow as it should look. Seven of the twelve frames are rebuilds of screens in the capture set — the clubs list, the four-step picker, the guest upsell, the fee screen, the membership-ID dialog, the success dialog and the club switcher. Four more are the states the capture set never recorded: no clubs yet, clubs loading, catalogue unavailable and card declined.

Third-party crests are the hard part of this flow, and it is the only flow where several of them appear at once. The current build puts them straight onto the card fill at four different sizes, so a yellow crest measures about 15:1 against the panel while a blue one drops to about 1.4:1 and dissolves. Every crest here sits on the crest plate settled in Club selection — a fixed light plate at one of three sizes, with the crest fitted to the padded square. We measure the plate, not the artwork, so the number is 14.4:1 on --surface for every club in the catalogue.

The second thing this flow gets wrong is that it never says which club is current. On the shipped list the only difference on the Barcelona card is that its Remove link is missing — a side effect standing in for a signal. Here the current club carries a Current pill, an accent edge, and a Remove control that is explicitly disabled with the reason written underneath it.

The third is the 3-club-per-league limit. It is stated in the shipped app as a three-line prose footnote in the lowest-contrast text on the screen, in two different wordings on two different screens, and never as a number. Here it is a counted readout with ticks, placed above the control it constrains, on both the list and the picker — and the clubs you have already used it on are named.

Provenance

What is reused and what is new

Reused from the library

  • Crest plate — the plate settled in club-selection.html, unchanged: fixed rgba(255,255,255,.94) ground, rgba(0,0,0,.13) inner hairline, .28 radius, .125 padding, contain-fit into the padded square, three sizes (28 / 40 / 64), code-plate fallback.
  • Cascade — the numbered, railed, locked four-step selector from club-selection.html, applied to the Profile entry point.
  • Dialog shell — dialogs.html verbatim: 20px insets, 340px cap, 20px padding, 44px icon tiles, two-up and stacked action rows, one scrim at rgba(3,4,1,.72) with a 10px blur.
  • Sheet shell — sheets.html verbatim: 18px top corners, grabber, hairline head, scrolling body, safe-area foot, and the .opt row with its four selection markers.
  • Control shell and toggle — forms.html verbatim: the 52px .ctl box, the label-outside-above convention, and the accent-on-track switch.
  • Limit readout — the counted ticks from club-selection.html.
  • Tap targets — the contract's ::after expansion on every sub-44 px control, so the sampled 38px and 34px visuals survive. Now also on sheets.html's 30px .sheet__x close disc, which frame 12 introduces to this file.
  • Frame 12, from the app itself — not from the library and not from the Figma. It is a straight rebuild of lib/pages/profile/club/add_club.dart, copy included, and it is the only frame here whose behaviour exists in code today.

New in this proposal

  • The entire add-a-club journey — frames 04 to 10. Not a rebrand. There is no code behind any of it: addUserClub (lib/service/src/club.dart:46) has zero callers, and there is no payment library, charge call or card capture anywhere in lib/. Building these seven frames is new product work, not restyling. Frame 12 is what replaces them today.
  • Current-club marker — a Current pill, an accent card edge, and a Make current action on the others. The shipped list identifies the current club only by the absence of its Remove link.
  • Three-rail club card — head, switch row, meta row. The shipped card puts name, switch and Remove on one 99pt rail with the switch floating near the horizontal centre, so nothing labels it.
  • Renewal consequence line — the switch sub-line names the renewal date and the amount when on, and the end date when off. The shipped screen says only Auto renewal.
  • Remove reason — the disabled Remove carries one line explaining why.
  • Empty state — frame 02. The shipped list has none; the profile screen it is reached from actually crashes for a zero-club account (see Decisions).
  • Loading state — frame 03, with the code plate as the crest placeholder so nothing reflows.
  • Catalogue-failure state — frame 05, scoped to the step that failed.
  • Declined state — frame 10. There is no failure counterpart to the success modal anywhere in the capture set.
  • Tier comparison table — six capability rows replacing two paragraphs of prose in which every word is capitalised. Yes/no is a tick against a dash.
  • Fee card — the amount and what it returns in one lockup. The shipped screen sets $30 ANNUAL REGISTRATION FEE as a caps heading with the amount and the descriptor at the same weight, and never puts the amount on the button.
  • Receipt in the success dialog — amount, card and credits restated. The shipped modal restates none of them.
  • Success mark — an acid tick tile on the crest. The shipped modal's only success signal is the word Successfully.
  • Blocked club rows — clubs you are already registered with are shown, locked and labelled, rather than silently absent.
  • League as metadata — leagues on their own line instead of concatenated into the club name string as BARCELONA (CL, LL).
  • Search that matches — the shipped filter strips spaces from the query but not from the club name, so manchester united matches nothing. Both sides are normalised here.
  • Sentence-case labels throughout — the folder splits 3–2 between Title Case and ALL CAPS button labels across seven screens. One convention here.
  • Plain-English step names — Competition level and League replace Jurisdictional level and League / Franchise, matching club-selection.html.

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. Do you want joining a club to work in the app at all?

    This is the biggest open question in the component, and frame 12 is the answer the code gives. Today the journey ends at a sheet that says "To add this club, kindly log in to sports heist on desktop" and an OKAY button that closes it (lib/pages/profile/club/add_club.dart:65-67 and :88). We re-checked every claim behind that before drawing it, and all of it holds:

    One — addUserClub (lib/service/src/club.dart:46), the only endpoint that would register a member with a club, has zero callers. The identifier appears exactly once in the whole of lib/, at its own declaration. Two — there is no payment library, no charge call and no card capture anywhere in the app. pubspec.yaml lists no payment or in-app-purchase dependency at all. What does exist is inert: lib/models/src/api.dart:346-347 and :374 mirror dj-stripe fields that nothing reads, Metadata.checkoutKey / checkoutCode (lib/models/src/api.dart:511-512) are parsed and never referenced outside the model, and ErrorResEnum.paymentError (lib/constants/src/enum.dart:223) resolves to the string 'credit_not_enough' (enum.dart:232-233) — it is an in-app participation-credit shortfall, not money. The Add Child Account flow says the same thing as the club flow: "To add a child account, please proceed to Sports Heist on Desktop".

    So frames 04 to 10 are a proposal for capability that does not exist, not a rebrand of something that does — and that is a budget question, not a design one. If the answer is that registration stays on desktop, then frame 12 is the screen to invest in and the right version of it hands off deliberately: a link that opens the desktop page with the club already chosen, rather than an OKAY that drops the member back where they started.

  2. Are the three defects in the shipped picker in scope?

    Frame 12 is reached through the PICK A CLUB sheet (lib/pages/profile/club/pick_club.dart:18), and we verified three faults in it that are cheap to fix and visible to any member:

    One — multi-word club names cannot be found. The filter at pick_club.dart:45-47 strips spaces from the query but not from the club name: club.name!.toLowerCase().contains(value.replaceAll(' ', '').toLowerCase()). Typing manchester united searches for manchesterunited against manchester united and returns nothing. Frame 04 normalises both sides. Two — the list is re-sorted during layout. pick_club.dart:172-181 calls .sort() on the club list from inside the horizontal ListView.builder's itemBuilder, so the collection is mutated once per item while the frame is being built — a source of items jumping under the finger and of index errors on long leagues. Three — a crest that fails to load takes the row with it. lib/pages/profile/widget/club_choosed.dart:50 renders the crest with Image.network and no errorBuilder and no loadingBuilder, so a broken or slow URL throws into the widget tree instead of degrading. (Note the path: the file is in pages/profile/widget/, not pages/profile/club/ where it is sometimes cited.)

    The third one the library already answers: the crest plate carries a code fallback that doubles as the loading placeholder, so a row never reflows and never depends on artwork arriving. We have assumed all three are fixed as part of this work. Confirm, or tell us to leave the picker alone.

  3. What can a guest actually not do?

    The guest screen promises that members unlock posting and liking ideas, voting on proposals and hosting locker rooms. The code enforces none of it. The only place the guest/member role is read at all is lib/pages/profile/profile_body.dart:127, and it is read purely to print a status label. Posting an idea is gated (lib/cubit/proposal/idea.dart:305) but by club-config flags and credit balance, identically for a guest and a member. Liking, voting and creating a locker room have no role check at any layer of the client. CLString.statusMember = ["MEMBER","GUEST"] (lib/constants/src/list_string.dart:3) has zero references, and ProfileS.statusMember (lib/cubit/profile/profile.dart:450) is declared, initialised to null, and never assigned — it is absent from copyWith, so it can never become anything else. We drew the four capability splits the upsell copy claims. Confirm the list is right, and confirm whether the gate is meant to be the tier or simply the credit balance — because today it is the credit balance, and a guest with credits can do everything.

  4. Which currency is $30?

    The fee renders as a bare $30 with no qualifier on the captured screen, and ConfigClub.creditAnnualMembershipCharge (lib/models/src/api.dart:772) is parsed but never read by any widget, so the code does not disclose one either. We rendered $30 unqualified. With Australian league data in the catalogue this needs settling before it ships.

  5. Should the profile screen survive a zero-club account?

    Today it does not. lib/cubit/profile/profile.dart:115-117 does indexWhere((element) => element.defaultClub == true) and then indexes the list with the result. If no club is flagged default — or if the account has no clubs at all — indexWhere returns -1 and resultsClub[-1] throws a RangeError before the screen can render. Immediately after, profile_body.dart:127 calls state.currentClub!.roles!.first, which throws again on an empty roles array. That is why no empty state was ever captured: the flow cannot reach one. We drew a real empty state on the assumption the fix is intended.

  6. Does switching the current club need a confirmation?

    Today it does not, and it also does not wait. change_club.dart:76-81 calls changeClubDefault(...) and then routes.pop() on the very next line, without awaiting it — so the sheet closes before the request resolves, and a failure has nowhere to surface. The cubit does show a loader (profile.dart:275-277), but by then the caller has already dismissed. Related: the sheet's BlocBuilder rebuilds only when currentTeamID changes (change_club.dart:57-58), so if the club list arrives after the sheet is open the sheet stays empty. We drew immediate commit with no confirmation, matching the shipped intent, but the sheet needs to stay open until the switch lands.

  7. Is auto renewal a real setting yet?

    ResultsClub.subscription (lib/models/src/api.dart:278) is a complete dj-stripe subscription mirror, including cancelAtPeriodEnd — the field an auto-renewal toggle would drive. It is parsed, serialised, and read by nothing, and no endpoint in lib/service/src/ writes to a subscription. So the toggle we drew on the list card and on the fee screen has no backing write path in this tree. We drew it as a live per-club setting, as the specs show. Confirm the write endpoint exists server-side.

  8. Is the 3-club limit per league, and is it enforced anywhere?

    The rule appears only as prose, in two different wordings, on two screens (CL-01 and CL-05), and nothing in lib/ counts, caps or mentions it. We built it as 3 registrations per league per season, counted and shown, with already-registered clubs locked in the picker. If the limit is per account rather than per league, the readout's label and the picker's locked rows both change.

  9. What is a participation credit worth?

    The captured screens state the same figure two ways — 30 Participation Credits on the fee screen and 30.0 worth of Participation Credits on the success modal, the second an unformatted float with the unit noun dropped. We rendered 30 as an integer, labelled credits back and participation credits added. If credits are fractional, the whole component's figures need decimals and a stated precision.
