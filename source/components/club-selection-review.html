What you're looking at

A cascade rebuilt so the dependencies show

Club selection exists in the current product as a stack of four dropdowns: sport, then competition level, then league, then club. Each one only works once the one above it is answered, but nothing on screen says so — a member taps a dead control and nothing happens. This build is new in the dark language and it fixes that by numbering the steps and railing them together: the rail above a step is accent once that step is satisfied and a hairline while it is not, and a step that cannot yet be answered carries a padlock and says what it is waiting for.

The second problem this screen solves is crests. Club artwork is authored for light grounds — kit, print, letterhead — and dropping it on a near-black card produced a tenfold contrast spread inside a single container in the current build: one club's yellow measured about 15:1 while another club's blue measured about 1.4:1 and effectively disappeared. Rather than measure each crest against our surface, every crest sits on an app-owned plate and we measure the plate. That number is then a constant for every club in the catalogue.

The third is the limit. A member may follow three clubs in any one league, and the limit is placed above the control it constrains rather than surfacing as an error after the tap.

Two devices sit above. The first is the screen itself: the cascade, the club list and the confirmed pick are one screen, because the list is revealed inside step 4 rather than navigated to. The second is the crest-plate specimen sheet — reference material, not a screen a member ever reaches, and now on its own device rather than trailing the product screen in one long scroll.

Provenance

What is reused and what is new

Reused from the Figma file

  • Tokens — surfaces, hairlines, accent, acid-ink, radii and the type scale, copied verbatim from the sampled set
  • Screen title — 34/800 uppercase at -.025em, left aligned
  • Section container — #080A0C, 1 px --line, R16, 12 px padding, used for the club panel
  • Status pill — the LIVE NOW geometry, type and dot, carrying the limit count
  • Icon button and pill button — 38 px circle, 34 px dark pill
  • Card and row — R16 card on #1C2129, rows with a --line-soft top border and none on the first
  • Selected-row emphasis — accent wash plus a 3 px edge, from the Merits leaderboard
  • Eyebrow — 10.5/700 tracked micro-label in #5B606B

New in this proposal

  • The whole screen — the current build is four unrelated dropdowns; there is no designed frame in this brand
  • Numbered, railed cascade — the dependency between the four steps is drawn, not implied
  • Locked-step language — padlock plus a sentence naming the blocker, on both the marker and the field
  • Crest plate — the ground, the bevel hairline, the 28% radius and 12.5% padding rule, the contain-fit, the three size steps and the derived radius/padding/type at each
  • Plate-not-crest contrast strategy — measuring an app-owned constant instead of third-party artwork
  • Ingest rule — reject artwork above 0.82 mean luminance over its alpha coverage and fall back to the code plate, rather than shipping a second plate tone
  • Code fallback — the short code as placeholder, error state and loading state in one
  • Inline option list — replaces the modal picker, so the answer lands next to the question
  • Three-club limit, promoted — pill, ticks, note and the already-following strip, all above the control they constrain
  • Pending tick — the provisional third slot at 45% accent, so a pick reads as not yet committed
  • Search across name and code — space-insensitive matching
  • Confirmation card — the pick restated with a 64 px plate and a Change escape
  • Docked action with a live note — the note always names the next missing answer, and disabled differs from enabled by opacity alone
  • Artwork stand-ins — four CSS blocks representing high-key stripes, low-key stripes, a pale keylined shield and a dark roundel. They are the only non-token colours in the file and they stand for artwork the app does not own; nothing in the product may reuse them

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. Where does crest artwork come from, and who checks it?

    No crest image assets exist. Every crest on this screen is either a code plate or one of four CSS stand-ins built to prove the plate contains real artwork. Before build we need a licensed source, a delivery format, and agreement that the ingest rule applies — reversed white-on-transparent marks get rejected and fall back to the code, because a light plate cannot hold an edge against them.

  2. Is a light plate acceptable in a dark app?

    It is the one place the product shows a light ground, and that is deliberate: crests are authored for light, so compositing them on light preserves the internal contrast their own designers signed off, and the app then takes responsibility only for plate-versus-surface, which it fully controls. The alternative is a tenfold contrast spread inside a single list, which is what the current build has. We need this agreed as a rule, not per screen.

  3. Are the three-letter codes ours to invent?

    Codes like BAR, RMA, BVB, SU58 and WI were written for this build. Some are conventional, some are guesses, and the plate allows 2–4 characters. They need to come from the catalogue rather than from us, because they appear wherever artwork is missing — which, today, is everywhere.

  4. Is the limit three per league, and what does "counts" mean?

    The build enforces three clubs in any one league and warns that the pick counts even if you leave mid-season. Both came from the spec and both need confirming. In particular: does leaving a club free the slot immediately, at the end of the season, or never? The note currently implies never, which is the strictest reading and the one that most needs to be right.

  5. Is the catalogue really this shape?

    Sport → competition level → league → club is four decisions to follow one team, and the International level puts the same clubs in more than one league — Real Madrid appears in the Champions League, the Club World Cup, La Liga and EuroLeague basketball. If a member following Barcelona in La Liga should automatically see them in Europe, the cascade is measuring competitions when the member is choosing a club, and step 2 may not need to exist.

  6. Can a member follow more than one club at all?

    The screen supports it — the limit, the already-following strip and the per-league counting all assume multiple clubs. But the intro says the pick becomes "your war room, your Backchat and your standings", singular. If following several clubs is real, every one of those surfaces needs a club switcher, and that is not designed.
