What you're looking at

One decision, wrapped in one number

Every Idea in SportsHeist is a race against two things at once: a threshold and a clock. It needs a set number of Likes, and it needs them before its window closes. Clear both and it becomes a Proposal that the whole club votes on. Miss either and it lapses, and the member who posted it loses their deposit. That is the entire proposition, and this screen exists to make it legible in about four seconds.

The live screen is the second-largest page file in the app — like_count.dart, 1,377 lines — and has never been captured beyond a single default-state screenshot. It renders on a white canvas with black text, which is not what the shipped app looks like. So this is the first time the screen has been drawn in the brand at all, and the first time its states have been drawn at any point.

The rebuild makes three changes worth flagging up front, all covered in Decisions below. It states the cost of a Like before the tap rather than only inside the confirming dialog. It gives a lapse a screen of its own, instead of a modal that shouts OOPS! and then walks the member out of the flow before they can read the outcome. And it lets the screen show what loaded when part of it fails, instead of showing nothing at all.

The progress chart is the piece most worth a careful look, because what it plots is not obvious and the live rendering obscures it. The Y axis is not the share of the membership that has liked the Idea. It is progress toward the number of Likes this particular club requires — so 100% is the threshold itself, and the "idea becomes proposal" marker necessarily sits on the top gridline. That is exactly what the code computes. The old build drew a decorative green band across the top 10% of the card, which happened to land near the right place without meaning anything.

Provenance

What is reused and what is new

Reused from the library

  • Token block — merits.html, copied verbatim. No new colour, no new radius.
  • Hero, verdict bar, subject card, 4-line clamp, READ MORE — action-detail.html, unchanged.
  • Ring gauge and Like CTA — idea-card.html, scaled from 64px to 96px and from card width to screen width. Same construction, same #3D424B track, same thumb pair.
  • Chart vocabulary — results-actions.html: .chart .ax .grid .axnum .thresh .threshlab .series .wdot .wtip .valnum.
  • Thread and composer — comments.html, including the 44px send disc and the counter that appears at 80 characters remaining.
  • Hold hatch — veto.html: rgba(155,226,34,.11) bars at 135°, 5px on / 6px off, over --sunken, with a rgba(155,226,34,.30) ring. Static.
  • Crest plate — club-selection.html, at the 28px dense-row size in its code-fallback form.
  • Empty and error panel — states.html, with its illustration rules: surface fill for context, dashed accent for something that should exist and does not yet.
  • Rows, amount lockup, deposit figure — create-idea.html and idea-gate.html. 25.0 Credits is the same number all three use.
  • 44px hit areas — the contract's sanctioned ::after expansion on every 38px and 36px disc.

New in this proposal

  • A chart with a real axis. The live axis ladders are Text widgets in a Stack spaced with MainAxisAlignment.spaceBetween, while the chart's own axis labels are set to Colors.transparent. So the visible ladders are decorative and are not guaranteed to align with the plotted points. Here the labels are anchored to the same coordinate space as the series.
  • The threshold on the top gridline. A dashed acid line at 100%, labelled with the actual figure. Replaces a fixed 320 × 0.1 green band pinned to the top of the card regardless of the data.
  • The count the percentage is of. 41 of 64 likes, and the derivation 5% of 1,284 members. The live screen shows a percentage and never the number.
  • The cost stated before the tap. Three rows saying free now / charged if it becomes a Proposal / never charged if it fails. The live screen puts all of this inside the dialog that opens after the member has already committed.
  • A lapsed screen. The live build has none: on the clock hitting zero it opens a modal headed OOPS! THIS IDEA HAS LAPSED and then pops the route twice, so the member never sees the outcome in place. Here the screen becomes the record — where it finished, and what happened to the deposit.
  • An author state. No Like button, and the deposit on screen. The live build has no author guard at all — it only hides the by <author> row when the viewer is the author, and draws the same Like CTA. Only the server can refuse it.
  • Remove a Like. The API already takes voteProposal(status: false); the screen only ever sends true. The pressed CTA toggles back.
  • Partial-failure rendering. Showing the two calls that succeeded instead of nothing at all.
  • Stage strip as a segmented rail. The four raster pictograms and the stack_header.png active marker have no dark-mode exports, so this is drawn: four 24-box glyphs at 1.8 stroke in an acid-wash pill.
  • One sentence instead of three lines. You liked this idea, replacing the You have / LIKED / this idea lockup.
  • Durations on the X axis. 2h 15m rather than a bare 2.25, and no 3 hour.
  • Nothing is pinned. Deliberate: the review page replays bottom chrome into every frame, which would put a Like button on the lapsed screen and the error screen. It also matches the live build, where the CTA and the composer are both inside the scroll view.

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. What does a Like cost, and when?

    The threshold and the deposit are both real config. The Like charge is not settled anywhere in the library. ConfigClub.credit_like_idea exists and is read on this screen, but no value has been agreed, and the string it feeds says the charge lands only once the Idea becomes a Proposal. We have shown 1.0 Credit, charged only on conversion across frames 01, 03 and 04. Confirm the figure, and confirm that it is charged on conversion rather than at the moment of liking — the whole cost explainer rests on that.

  2. Is the like threshold ever weighted?

    Today it is flat: target_percentage_like × total_member, both from the club config. But the config object also parses three fields that nothing in the app reads — target_percentage_like_with_early_influencers, ..._with_leading_influencers and ..._with_emerging_influencers. That looks like a planned rule where an Idea backed by influential members needs fewer Likes. We have built the flat threshold and said so on screen: 5% of 1,284 members = 64 Likes. If the weighted version is coming, the screen needs a way to say "the bar moved, and here is why", and the chart's Y scale changes underneath it.

  3. Should the author be blocked from liking their own idea, in the client?

    We have assumed yes — no Like control at all for the author, on the grounds that a member should not be able to buy their own deposit back. The live build has no such guard: it draws the same button and relies on the server to refuse. If the server does not refuse it, the author currently can like their own Idea, and that is a rule question, not a UI one.

  4. Can a member remove a Like?

    The API accepts it. The screen never offers it. We have made the CTA a toggle while the Idea is still in the Ideas stage, matching idea-card.html. Confirm that is allowed, and confirm what happens if a removal drops the count back below the threshold after it has been crossed.

  5. What happens the instant the threshold is crossed?

    Today, nothing on this screen: the code detects the crossing, refetches the history, waits three seconds and then pushReplacementNameds the member onto the Voting screen. So there is no "it made it" moment — the screen the member was reading is replaced under them. We have not built that state, because it needs a decision: a confirmation on this screen with a deliberate move to Voting would be our recommendation, rather than an automatic redirect after an arbitrary delay.

  6. Is a lapse always a forfeit, including for a scheduled or short window?

    The settled rule is that a lapse is the one case where the 25.0 Credit deposit is forfeited, and frame 08 states it plainly. Worth confirming the edges: an Idea whose window outruns the fixture is cancelled and refunded, and a scheduled Idea cancelled before it posts is refunded — so the forfeit only applies to an Idea that actually ran and fell short. That is what we have written.

  7. Does the description need images or links?

    Built as plain paragraphs with a 4-line clamp, matching the live field. If members are expected to attach a photograph or link to a fixture report, the subject card needs a media slot and the clamp needs to account for it.

  8. Sorting and moderation in the discussion

    The live comment list sorts newest-first and offers no other order, no replies and no report path. comments.html proposes most-agreed sorting, one level of replies, and an overflow menu on every comment. We have shown the thread newest-first with the overflow present and left the sort control off this screen. Confirm whether the War Room thread gets the full comments.html behaviour or the reduced one.
