Foundations

The complete surface of the SportsHeist dark-mode rebrand: four surfaces, one accent, one semantic red, twelve type steps, three radii, fourteen controls, sixteen glyphs. Every value here is the value in merits.html and idea-card.html, which were sampled from War Room-Dark Mode.svg and Backchat-Dark Mode.svg. Contrast ratios are computed from the hex values using the WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula, not estimated.

Two badges run through the whole page and they are the point of it: Sampled means the value or component exists in the Figma export and has already shipped in a component file. Proposed means it does not exist yet — it was built here from the established language and needs a design decision before anyone relies on it.

Sampled

Four steps, all near-black. The ladder is built from luminance, never from a new hue.

The ladder, nested as it actually stacks

#030401--bg · screen canvasY 0.108%
#080A0C--section · 1 px --line, R16, 12 px paddingY 0.295% · +0.187 pp on --bg
#1C2129--surface · card, R16, 16 px paddingY 1.495% · +1.199 pp on --section
#13171E--sunken · inner panel, R12Y 0.845% · −0.650 pp on --surface

--sunken is a recess, not a rise. It is darker than --surface, so the nesting order (bg → section → surface → sunken) is not the luminance order (bg → section → sunken → surface). A sunken panel only ever appears inside a --surface card, where the −0.650 pp drop reads as a well. On --section or --bg it would read as a raised block and must not be used there.

White never sits on the accent. 1.58:1 fails every threshold including the 3:1 floor for non-text UI. Accent fills always take --acid-ink #0B0E06; that pairing is what the export uses on the active tab pill and the Like button, and it clears AAA at 12.31:1.

03

Semantic

Sampled

One hue, for downward movement and destruction. That is the entire semantic layer.

There is no other colour in this system. No silver, no bronze, no blue, no purple, no amber warning state, no separate info blue. Upward and positive is --acid #9BE222. Downward and destructive is #E2564A. Everything else is a surface, a hairline or a grade of white. If a design needs a distinction, it comes from scale, weight, ring opacity or the accent wash — never from a new hue.

Three radii carry the system. 12 and 16 never mix on the same edge: a 12 px panel always sits inside a 16 px card with at least 12 px of padding between them, so the curves never fight. 999px is used rather than 50% on anything that can hold a label, so the shape survives a longer string. The only other measured radius in the export is the 5 px on the 17 px .crest square.

06

Controls

Fourteen controls, every state each one has. Six are sampled from the export; eight are proposed and need sign-off.

State vocabulary. Only three hover colours exist and all three are sampled: --acid → #A8EC33, --surface → #222833, --sunken → #1A1F27. Pressed is translateY(1px) — sampled from .sh-like:active — plus, on this page, a Proposed rgba(0,0,0,.18) veil over the base token so the press is visible without a screenshot; no new hex enters the system. Disabled is a Proposed opacity:.38 on the base token plus cursor:not-allowed — it lands at about 2.6:1 against its own surface, deliberately below the 3:1 interactive floor so it cannot be mistaken for live. Focus is outline:2px solid var(--acid); outline-offset:3px — sampled from .sh-like:focus-visible. Nothing in the Figma export shows a disabled or focused control; every disabled and focus specimen below is proposed even where the control itself is sampled.

Icons

Inline SVG only. 24-box viewBox, round caps and joins, no fills except the one solid thumb. Each glyph is shown at 24 px and 18 px.

Every glyph below is written out in full as a 24-box inline SVG at its 24 px size — copy that node. The 18 px duplicate references the same symbol, which is the point: one geometry, two sizes, no separate small set. Stroke weight is not uniform — see the note under the utility grid.

Stroke weight is not uniform in the export, and the contract's 1.7–1.9 range is not the whole truth. The tab glyphs are 1.7. Search, filter and timer are 1.9. But plus is 2.4, the delta arrows are 2.8 in the standing card and 3.2 in the leaderboard row, and the empty-state swords are 1.5. The pattern underneath is optical, not fixed: the smaller the render, the heavier the stroke, so the glyph keeps its weight at 10 px. The five proposed glyphs follow the same rule — 1.8–1.9 at the 17–24 px sizes they are drawn for, and the checkbox tick is thickened to 2.2 because it renders at 9 px. Worth deciding whether the contract should say 1.5–3.2, scaled optically instead.
