What credits are for

Posting an idea takes a deposit
Every idea you post holds a participation deposit of 25.0 Credits. The deposit leaves your available balance and sits on deposit while the idea is live.
You get the deposit back when it becomes a Proposal
Returned the moment your idea gathers enough Likes — and if the idea is cancelled because the match ended. Whatever the Proposal goes on to be costs you nothing.
One case loses it: the idea lapses
If the Ideas stage ends without enough Likes, the deposit of 25.0 Credits is forfeited. That is the only way a deposit does not come back.
Voting in Support costs credits
Liking an idea is free. When the idea becomes a Proposal your Like converts to a vote in Support for 1.0 Credit.
Credits are not Merits
Credits are a balance — bought, deposited, spent, returned. Merits are earned standing: never spent, never bought. A vote in Support earns Merits; Merits never buy anything.
