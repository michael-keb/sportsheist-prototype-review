What you're looking at

Eight surfaces that narrow a list

Every list in the War Room is filtered before it is read. Two of those filters — the ones on Results and Actions — have never been designed, captured or specified. They are opened by a small circular button at the head of the category strip, and what opens is a sheet whose three rows do nothing at all: each row's tap handler in the shipped code is an empty function. The sheet can be scrolled and admired; it cannot sort.

So this file does two jobs. It rebuilds the surfaces that exist — the sort sheet, the date picker, the date range, the explainer popover — on the dark kit, and it fills the three holes that make those surfaces unusable: there is no way to tell how many filters are on, no way to clear them, and nothing on screen when a filter matches nothing. Filtering a list to zero results in today's build produces a blank area under the chips.

Nothing here is in the Figma file. Every construction is borrowed from a component that is: the sheet shell, option rows, indicators and calendar grid are sheets.html unchanged; the screen title, taxonomy switch, category chips, result cards and verdict plates are results-actions.html unchanged; the nothing-matched panel is ideas-list.html's. What is genuinely new is the count on the filter trigger, the summary line that says 2 filters on · 3 of 14 with Clear all beside it, the filter sheet itself, and the nothing-matched copy.

States

What varies

StateTreatment
No filter All chip pressed, trigger plain with no ring and no count, no summary line at all. Absence of the line is the signal.
Filter on Three signals at once: a tick inside the accent chip, an accent ring plus a numeral on the trigger, and the summary line in words with Clear all. Any one of the three could be missed; all three cannot.
Filter matches nothing The dashed panel, the two colliding filters named in the sentence, and a button that clears them. The summary line still reads 2 filters on · 0 of 14 so the member knows the list is not empty, the filter is.
Stage genuinely empty Not this panel. No results yet on the solid section edge, with no button — there is nothing to undo. The build uses one string, 'No {} yet', for both cases and shows it only on the War Room rails.
Sort row selected Round indicator filled with an accent dot, row washed at 12%, rim at 30%, label accent and 600. Exactly one row in the group.
Filter row checked Square indicator filled #9BE222 with an #0B0E06 tick. Any number of rows. Head count and Apply count both track it live, and Clear all disables at zero.
Day unselectable #5B606B and struck through, cursor not-allowed. Future days on both pickers, because lastDate: DateTime.now(). Next month is disabled rather than removed, so the edge of the window is visible instead of mysterious.
Today A 1 px accent ring that survives under a selected disc, since both ends of a range usually sit near today.
Range incomplete The unset field reads Choose a date in #5B606B at 500; Apply stays available only once both ends exist. Setting From after To is prevented by the grid, not by an error message.
Explainer open Trigger disc inverts — accent fill on a plain half, #0B0E06 fill on the selected half — and the popover appears with its tail under the disc. Escape, the ✕ or a second tap closes it.
Explainer with no copy Unbuilt on purpose. Positions has no explainer text anywhere in the codebase, and a popover containing filler is worse than a disc that is not there.

New in this proposal

  • A sort sheet that sorts — every row in both shipped sheets carries onPress: () {}
  • A filter sheet — the app has none; its filter-glyph trigger opens a sort list
  • Verdict as a filter — you cannot currently narrow Results to Approved at all
  • The count on the filter trigger and the accent ring around it
  • The filter summary line — 2 filters on · 3 of 14 results — and Clear all, which does not exist in any form today
  • A tick inside the active chip, so the state is not carried by hue alone
  • The nothing-matched panel — the build renders a blank area
  • One range surface instead of a dialog opening a dialog
  • Struck-through unselectable days and a disabled Next month, so the window is legible
  • A dismissible explainer with a title, a tail and a 44px trigger
  • English throughout. Every string on the range surface is Vietnamese in the build

Five faults are corrected here rather than reproduced, and each is worth reading as a bug report. One — both sort sheets are inert: result/widget/show_filter.dart:53 and action/widget/show_filter.dart:53 pass an empty function as the row handler, while the identical sheets on Ideas and Voting pass a real one. Two — the same sheet is built at two row heights: Actions sets height: CSpace.height * 0.08 (action/widget/show_filter.dart:49), Results leaves it unset. Three — neither list has a zero branch, so a filter that matches nothing shows nothing (result_screen.dart:372, action_screen.dart:379), even though the app already owns EmptyContainer. Four — the filter survives a screen change visually but not actually: initState re-requests the default unfiltered list (result.dart:172) without clearing the selection state, so you return to green chips over unfiltered results. Five — the date layer is pinned to Vietnamese in an English-only app: Locale('vi') at select_date/index.dart:102 and :130, DateFormat(… , 'vi') at on_tap.dart:392 and :405, and hardcoded Vietnamese labels at on_tap.dart:149, :163, :233, :252 and :293.

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. Should the taxonomy and the categories be independent?

    They are not today. getFilterListIdea sends a category as the type parameter with category: null (cubit/proposal/result.dart:52–61), so choosing On-field silently discards your Decisions / Positions choice — and switching to Positions shrinks the chip list from six entries to three while keeping the numeric index, which turns My ideas into On-field without telling you. We built them as two independent axes, which is what the sheet implies and what a member will expect. If the API cannot carry both at once, the chips have to become part of the taxonomy rather than sit beside it, and the sheet shrinks.

  2. What can actually be filtered on the server?

    getListIdea sends exactly five query parameters — club_uuid, stage, category, type, ordering — plus limit: '50' (service/src/proposal.dart:52–59). There is no status parameter and no is_vote parameter, so the Verdict and Mine groups cannot be server-side today. We built them filtering the page already in memory, which is defensible only because the list is a single request capped at 50 and therefore complete for most clubs. If a club can hold more than 50 settled Results, those two groups need real query parameters or they will lie.

  3. Is one filter enough per category, or many?

    The chips are single-select today: tapping one replaces the last. We built the sheet as multi-select with Apply and kept the chips as single-select shortcuts to the same state, so the chip strip stays a one-tap path and the sheet is where combinations are made. The alternative — chips that also multi-select — makes the strip state harder to read at 375 px and needs a horizontal scroll indicator.

  4. Should a filter survive leaving the screen?

    Today it half-survives, which is the worst of both: the chips remember, the list forgets. We assumed the filter is per-visit and resets on entry — so the chips must reset too. The other defensible answer is that a filter is a member preference and persists until cleared, which needs it written to the cubit's initial state and re-applied on initState. Either is fine; the current mixture is not.

  5. What does the Positions explainer say?

    There is one real explainer sentence in the whole codebase — listExplainTitleIDea[0], about On-field Decisions — and it is used on one screen that is not this one. Its sibling for Positions is lorem ipsum, and all four War Room list screens pass lorem ipsum directly at the call site. We shipped the Decisions popover only and left the Positions disc without one. Two or three sentences on what a Position is and we will build it.

  6. Which date does the range filter, and does it exist?

    The range dialog in on_tap.dart and the SelectDate widget are both dead code — nothing in lib/ constructs either, and the only live date-range field sits in an admin user-list drawer with no route into it. We built the range as Settled between, filtering on the date a verdict was reached, because that is the only date a Result carries that a member would search by. If the range is meant to filter Actions by kick-off, or if it is not wanted at all, say so — it is the one surface here with no live caller.

  7. Monday-first weeks?

    The grid starts on Monday, which suits AU and EU and is wrong for a US audience. The build never decided, because it never drew a calendar — it asked the platform, in Vietnamese. If the app ships to both, the grid must follow the device locale.

  8. Can we delete the Vietnamese?

    assets/translations/vi.json still ships, main.dart declares supportedLocales: [Locale("en")], and seven date helpers in utils/src/convert_data.dart plus both date pickers hardcode 'vi'. We built English only. Confirm the app is English-only and we will treat every Vietnamese string and every hardcoded 'vi' locale as a defect to be removed, not a feature to be preserved.
