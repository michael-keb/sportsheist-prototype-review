The bottom-sheet kit: one panel that rises from the bottom of the screen, and the seven jobs it does — sort, filter, pick one of many, pick a date, pick a time, offer a short list of actions, and confirm something irreversible. It replaces four different sheet constructions, two hard-coded sheet heights and one raw Material dropdown currently shipping in the app.

What you're looking at

One panel, seven jobs, one set of rules

A bottom sheet is the phone's answer to "choose something without leaving the page". The Backchat sort and filter sheets in the dark-mode export already establish the language — a dark panel with rounded top corners, a small tracked uppercase title on a hairline, full-width rows with the label left and the indicator right, the chosen row washed in a dark green tint, and a full-width accent Apply where one is needed. This file takes that language and finishes it.

Everything below is the same shell. What changes between the seven is what sits in the scrolling middle and whether the foot carries a button. Nothing else — not the radius, not the grabber, not the scrim, not the height rule — varies. That is the point: today the app ships sheets at 330 pt, 470 pt and 90%, one of them with square top corners and no grabber at all, and each one styled at the call site.

The page is laid out as specimens so all seven can be read at once. Three live examples at the foot behave properly: they slide up over the real scrim, hold keyboard focus inside themselves, and close on a scrim tap, a downward drag of the grabber, the ✕ or Esc. The Category sheet is the Hall of Fame surface rebuilt — the shipped version has no top radius and no grabber.

Provenance

What is reused and what is new

Reused from the Figma file

  • Sheet construction — dark panel, rounded top corners, grabber, slide-up over a dimming scrim, from the Backchat sort / filter / search sheets
  • Small tracked uppercase title on a hairline — the export's sheet-title treatment, re-set to the contract's 11 / 700 / .14em
  • Row with label left, indicator right — the export's option row
  • Selected row washed in the accent with an accent indicator — the export's selected state, recoloured from blue to #9BE222
  • Full-width accent Apply — the export's filter-sheet footer
  • Checkbox and radio — the Forms kit, unchanged
  • Control shell for the search field — the Forms kit, unchanged
  • Action pair, 46 px pill buttons — the Dialogs kit, unchanged
  • Scrim at 72% plus a 10 px blur — the Dialogs kit, unchanged
  • Sort and filter option names — the shipped UpcomingLockerRoomsSort and LockerRoomsFilter enums
  • Time offsets — the shipped create-room presets
  • Award category names — Member of the Match / Week / Season
  • Clubs — the club-selection data

New in this proposal

  • One height rule — min(88vh, 560px) with a fixed head and foot, replacing hard-coded 330 / 470 / 90%
  • Radius and grabber on every sheet — including the Hall of Fame category sheet, which ships with neither
  • Safe-area behaviour — nothing in the export or the build reserves the home indicator; HF-03's last row currently ends inside the gesture zone
  • Radio for single-select — the export draws checkboxes on the single-select sort rows
  • Active-filter count and Clear all — no applied-filter marker exists anywhere in the build
  • Date picker in the brand — the build themes a third-party table_calendar inline at the call site
  • Range variant — no range selection exists
  • Time picker — the build has date and offset presets but no time surface
  • Action sheet — with a context block and exactly one destructive item
  • Confirm sheet — the sheet-form sibling of the dialog
  • Correctly anchored dropdown — flush left, 6 px below its trigger, siblings inert
  • Focus trap, Esc, drag-to-dismiss — none of the three exist in the build
  • Grouped filter rows — Access / Me / Sport, so a multi-select list is readable

Two faults recorded in the specs are fixed rather than reproduced. HF-03 — the Hall of Fame category sheet — has top corner radius 0 and no drag handle, against the 24 pt radius the component reference records for sheets; it also puts black rows on a lighter panel, so the interactive elements are darker than the surface they sit on. And on HF-02 the Season field opens the stock Material dropdown, whose left edge sits 4 pt right of the field's left edge and whose top edge sits 6 pt above the field's top edge, so it covers its own trigger, swallows the whole Category field below it and clips the podium's corner. Both are rebuilt here.

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. Sort rows: checkbox or radio?

    The export draws the sort options as rows with a square checkbox tick, but only one sort order can be active. A checkbox says "tick as many as you like" and then refuses to let you. We built a radio, and used the square checkbox only on the genuinely multi-select filter sheet, so the indicator shape now tells you the rule before you tap. Confirm and we will hold that distinction everywhere.

  2. Is a locked room "closed" or "locked"?

    The shipped enum label is Closed rooms only; the room type it filters on is locked, the room card renders Locked room with a padlock, and one export writes Locked rooms only. We used Locked rooms only because it matches the padlock the user already sees. One word, product-wide, please.

  3. Does "All rooms" survive multi-select?

    The shipped filter list leads with All rooms alongside four real filters, which cannot work once more than one can be on. We treated nothing ticked as "all rooms" and dropped the row, which is what the count pill and Clear all now express. If All rooms must stay as a visible reset, it becomes a text button, not an option.

  4. What is a season actually called?

    The Hall of Fame Season control is documented as three rows with no strings recorded, and not one date on any Hall of Fame screen carries a year — on a surface whose filter is the year. We used 2025/26 · 2024/25 · 2023/24. Tell us the real identifiers, and whether a season is a calendar year or a split year.

  5. Should Season stay a dropdown at all?

    Our rule: a sheet for more than about six options, anything multi-select, anything needing search or an Apply; a dropdown only for three to six short single-line values changing one field in place. Season passes as a dropdown and Category passes as a sheet, so we kept the split and made both legible. The alternative — both as sheets — is more consistent and one tap slower. Your call.

  6. Monday-first weeks and quarter-hour times?

    The date grid starts on Monday and the time picker offers quarter hours only. Monday-first suits AU and EU and is wrong for US audiences; if the app ships to both, the grid needs to follow the device locale rather than a fixed order. And if a locker room may start at 8:37, the minute column needs every value and gets noticeably harder to use.

  7. What replaces the sheet on desktop?

    These are phone surfaces. On the desktop shell a sheet rising from the bottom of a 1440-wide window is wrong. We would expect the filter and sort sheets to become anchored panels beside their trigger and the pickers to become modals, but the desktop frames in the export do not show either, so that is unbuilt and unsized.
