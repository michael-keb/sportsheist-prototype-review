What is real and what is proposed

Real
The verdict, the subject, the reason, every date and the vote count. All of it is already on the record MProposal returns from GET /proposals/{uuid}/ — including parent_id, the link back to the originating idea, which nothing in the live build reads.
Missing
Follow-through does not exist in the data. No owner, no due date, no completion flag, no evidence, and no endpoint to record any of them. The one step this screen cannot fill is the one the screen is for, so it is drawn as an unresolved dashed mark rather than faked as a progress bar.
Proposed
Three fields, marked Proposed wherever they appear: owner, committed_by and confirmed_at. Add those three and the loop closes.

States · five of the seven verdicts reach this screen

Reaches
Approved · Ruled out · Vetoed · Veto hold · Cancelled. An action exists because a proposal passed, so the verdict here is either the pass or something that struck it out afterwards.
Cannot
Rejected ends at the Result — a proposal members voted down never becomes an action. Lapsed ends at the Idea stage, before there is a proposal at all. Neither is drawn here; a link to either should land on the Result detail instead.
Live build
Ships two: rule_outed in red, everything else in green. And func.dart::findStatusAction matches case "rule_outed " with a trailing space, so an approved action currently renders to members as Ruled out, in red.
