Text chat

The live text lane that runs beside the audio while a locker room is open. Ephemeral, fast, many short messages, read once and in order — not the threaded argument in Comments, and not the audio stage in Locker room.

Not a thread
Order is the only structure. No replies, no indent, no sort control, no “most agreed”. A chat you can re-order is a comment thread with a worse composer.
Not a ballot
Nothing here is counted. Comments carries agree / disagree as ink; chat does not carry agreement at all. No tallies, no percentages, no thresholds.
One accent fill
The send button, and nothing else. Your own messages, the host, the club and the unread divider all use accent as ink or a hairline — never as a fill.
In the code
None of this exists yet. chat_body.dart is the Backchat home scaffold — room lists and a refresh. There is no message model, no composer and no chat socket anywhere in the repository.

    Not sent · no connection

The text stays white and full-contrast — a failure is loud, not disabled. Red appears only on the 12 px glyph and the 1 px hairline, both at or above the 3:1 non-text floor, and it is paired with the words Not sent either way. Retry re-sends in place and keeps the original position in the log; Delete removes it with no confirmation, because nothing has left the device.

Jump to latest

Sits in the pinned layer above the composer, so it never scrolls away and never lands on a message. It appears once the log is more than one screen from the foot, and carries the unread count when there is one — with no count when the member simply scrolled up. The log auto-scrolls only when already at the foot; it never yanks the view while somebody is reading back.

Composer

Comments' composer, unchanged: 12 px radius, --surface fill, the accent border and 3 px --acid-wash focus ring every field in the kit uses, 16 px value text, a 44 px minimum height and a 44 px accent send disc. Four things are tuned for a live surface — the growth cap drops to 68 px (three lines), the limit to 300 characters, the counter appears at 40 remaining, and Return sends while Shift+Return makes a newline.
