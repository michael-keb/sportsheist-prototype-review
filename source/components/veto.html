A veto is not a rejection. Club Management can hold a decision only where carrying it out would break a contract, a competition rule or the law. It cannot hold one because it disagrees with the outcome, and it has to publish its reason, signed, to every member.

You can put it back to the club. An Idea to Reject the Veto runs through the normal Ideas and Voting stages. Reach 80% Support and the veto is removed — the club overrules its own management, and this proposal carries on where it left off.
