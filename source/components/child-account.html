Step 7 of 7 The identity check

Your consent

SportsHeist has a one-fan, one-membership rule — it prevents fraud and protects the value of every vote — so every member's identity is checked before a membership is issued. Thanh is 15, so you consent on their behalf. Read what that involves.

What is collected

  • Already given, by you Thanh's name, date of birth, email address, display name and your relationship to them.
  • For the check itself A photo of one identity document in Thanh's name — passport, driving licence or national ID card — and a selfie of Thanh taken on this phone at the same time. Nothing is taken from your camera roll.
  • What we never ask for Their school, their home address, their phone number, or a payment card in their name. Spending runs through your card only.

Who checks it

GBG is the identity-verification provider SportsHeist uses. The check runs on GBG's own secure page at identify.idscan.cloud, which opens inside this app. GBG sees the document and the selfie and returns a pass or a fail. It does not see the rest of your SportsHeist account, your password or your card.

How long it is kept

  • The document photo and the selfie — 30 days Held by GBG, then deleted. SportsHeist never receives the images.
  • The result — 7 years Pass or fail, the date, and the type of document. We have to be able to show that every member was age-checked before a membership was issued.
  • Name, date of birth and relationship — while the account exists Deleted 90 days after you close the account, or 90 days after Thanh turns 18 and takes it over.
  • Never shown to other fans Their age, date of birth and your relationship stay between you, them and us. Backchat and Merits show a display name only.
