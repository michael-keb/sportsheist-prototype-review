The screen where a member actually casts the vote the whole product exists for. It is the detail behind the Voting card: the proposal in full, the clock, the threshold, the ranked ballot, the count once it is running, and every way the count can end — approved, rejected, held under a veto, or cancelled by the club.

What you're looking at

One screen, ten states, one ballot idiom

This is a rebuild of the largest page in the app — lib/pages/voting/vote_count.dart, 1,493 lines — which has never been redesigned. It is where a proposal is read, argued over and decided, so it carries more state than any other screen: before the vote, mid-ballot, after the vote with the count locked, after the vote with the count running, on veto hold, approved, rejected, cancelled, empty and failed.

Nothing about the ballot is new here. The threshold bar, the ranked option rows and the rank plate are the ones already signed off on the Voting card, shown at full scale instead of squeezed into a 268 px rail card. The verdict bar and the count chart come from Results & actions. The discussion thread and the pinned composer come from Comments. The hatch, the hold banner and the struck bar fill come from Veto. There is exactly one way to express a ballot in this library and this screen uses it.

Three things in the live build are fixed rather than reproduced. The build's ranked ballot ships an instruction banner telling members that clicking numbers the choices, and then renders no rank affordance at all — no number, no badge, no handle, nothing to indicate the row is even tappable. Here every row carries a visible numbered slot, filled or empty, and the numbers appear in it. The build's post-vote receipt prints one uppercased word (PEP) in a centred lockup, 60 pt above a tally that renders the same name in title case, with no link between them and every rank stripped out; here the member's ballot is restated in full, in order, with its plates intact. And the build's chart-gate panel reads Current: 1 votes — the plural noun against the quantity one, with no pluralisation anywhere in the string table. That is now a pip row and a sentence.

Provenance

What is reused and what is new

Reused, unchanged in kind

  • Threshold bar — track, accent fill, marker knob and threshold tick from voting-card.html, scaled from 8 to 10 px
  • Ranked option row and rank plate — voting-card.html, scaled from 25 to 28 px, with the same accent wash and 3 px inset bar for a ranked row
  • Verdict bar — results-actions.html, four tones and two hues, at its --lg detail scale
  • Count chart panel — results-actions.html's SVG chart, its axis classes, its threshold dash and its intersection-triggered draw-in
  • Tally rows — results-actions.html's swatch / name / value row
  • Thread, agree-disagree pills and composer — comments.html, including the rule that agreement is ink, never a fill
  • The hold treatment — veto.html's --hold-bars, --hold-ring and --struck, copied token for token
  • Clock tiles — veto.html, including the rule that a held clock keeps white digits
  • Buttons and controls at 52 px — forms.html
  • Hit-area expansion — the contract's ::after{inset:-3px} on every 38 px and 34 px disc

New in this proposal

  • A visible rank affordance — every row carries a numbered slot. The build describes clicking to number the choices and renders nothing at all
  • The member's own ballot, restated in order — replacing a single uppercased word floating 60 pt above an unlinked tally. Eliminated preferences take the contract's dashed plate
  • The which-number line — one sentence separating the redistributed leading share from the first-preference rows. Two percentages that mean different things previously sat together unlabelled
  • Turnout as its own figure — a clock tile reading 1,079 of 1,412 eligible · 76% turnout, so the chart is free to plot support
  • The gate as a pip row — 3 of 5 votes cast with five pips, replacing Chart will appear after 5 votes / Current: 1 votes, a ghosted watermark and a white keyline. Pluralisation fixed
  • A frozen chart — the series turns dashed and stops at the freeze instead of the panel simply vanishing
  • An error state — the build has none; a failed fetch shows an internal-error dialog over an empty screen
  • An honest empty state — em-dashes rather than 0% where no vote has been cast, so nothing looks like a measured zero
  • The action pair clears the composer — in the build the pinned composer overlays the Cancel / Vote buttons and only ~10 pt of them is reachable
  • Cost and refund copy on the ballot — the build hides the price behind a confirmation dialog after the member commits

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. What is the real quorum before the chart opens?

    Chart will appear after 5 votes and Current: 1 votes appear in the screenshots and nowhere else — zero matches across lib/ and en.json, and ConfigClub has no minimum-vote field. In the supplied code there is no gate at all: ChartCommon renders as soon as getListChart returns, and that function has an explicit branch for an empty vote history. We have reproduced the gate at 5 votes, hard-coded. It should become a club-config threshold beside the five existing vote targets, and we need to know whether it counts votes or distinct voters.

  2. Should the chart plot support or turnout?

    The live chart is handed y = voted / count_member_participants — that is turnout — and then draws a green APPROVE band at the approval threshold. Those are two different quantities on one plate: a proposal with 100% turnout and 20% support would draw a line deep inside the approve band. We have plotted support share against the band, because that is what the band means, and moved turnout to its own figure above the chart. Confirm, because it changes the API the chart reads.

  3. Is the headline percentage the redistributed share or first preferences?

    The Voting card shows 74% against a 70% threshold while its ballot rows read 34 / 28 / 22 / 16. Both can be true — one is the leading option after eliminations, the other is first preferences — but nothing says so. We have assumed the bar measures the leading option after redistribution and added a line saying it. If the bar is meant to measure first preferences, the verdict pill is wrong on both screens.

  4. Is the ballot ranked or binary?

    The supplied code implements a binary Support / Reject vote: voteProposal(status: true|false), one boolean, and a chart with APPROVE / REJECT bands. The screenshots implement ranked-choice with instant runoff and a Reject all options row. They cannot both be right. We have built ranked choice, because that is the newer build and it is what the Results screen's round-by-round chart already assumes — but the vote endpoint in this checkout cannot carry a ranking.

  5. Can a member vote without ranking every option?

    The build's instruction says to rank until all are numbered, so Vote stays disabled until the ballot is complete. If a partial ranking is valid, the button should enable on the first rank and we need to know what happens to the blank rows in the final round.

  6. Which option set is canonical?

    The library now ships two for the same proposal: voting-card.html lists three real managers by name plus Reject all options, and results-actions.html lists six role-based routes. We have used the role-based set here so the detail, the result and the round chart agree, and because naming real individuals on a live appointment ballot is a legal question, not a design one. The rail card needs bringing onto whichever set wins.

  7. Should the screen redirect itself when the clock hits zero?

    The build calls onDoneNavigatorAction at Duration.zero and pushReplacementNameds straight to the Result — so a member mid-sentence in the composer loses the screen without warning. We have not drawn that. Our recommendation is an in-place transition to the settled state with the count frozen and a See the result CTA, never an automatic route change.

  8. The composer appears on frames it should not

    Bottom-pinned chrome has to live outside <main> per the contract, and the review page replays it into every frame — so the composer also shows on Couldn't load and, arguably, on Cancelled. In the real build it should be hidden when the proposal failed to load and disabled once the proposal is cancelled or vetoed. Confirm the second one: a veto hold is exactly when members most want to argue.

  9. Is the shimmer worth 180 lines?

    vote_count.dart spends roughly 180 of its 1,493 lines hand-building a grey placeholder for this one screen. We have not drawn a loading state because the library's shared skeleton should serve it. Confirm we can retire the bespoke one.

  10. Two library-wide items this screen inherits

    Control height is still open: 52 px here, from forms.html, against auth.html's better-sourced 64 px. And the 34 px/38 px discs keep their measured size with the invisible 44 px hit-area expansion rather than growing — the ::after pattern, applied consistently. Both need settling once, for the whole library, not per screen.
