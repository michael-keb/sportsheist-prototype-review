Three steps, then straight back

  1. Photograph the document Passport, driving licence or national ID card, in Thanh's name. JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 7.5 MB.
  2. Take Thanh's selfie On this phone, straight after the document, with them present. GBG compares the two.
  3. You land back here GBG returns you to SportsHeist and we show the result on this screen. Most come back in under a minute; if a person has to review it, we email you within one working day.
