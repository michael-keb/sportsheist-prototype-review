1 · Sort

Single-select · applies on tap

Sort upcoming locker rooms

Discrepancy, and what we built. The export draws these three rows with a square checkbox tick, but the behaviour is single-select — one sort order at a time. A checkbox promises "tick as many as you like". Built with a radio. Round indicator, one selected, tapping a row applies the sort and closes the sheet, so there is no Apply button here. The three orders are the shipped UpcomingLockerRoomsSort values; the live list only exposes two of them.

7 · Confirm

Same shell, centred content, the dialogs kit's 46 px action pair in the foot. Use the sheet form when the confirm follows a sheet — an action sheet should not hand off to a centre-screen dialog — and the dialog form when it follows a screen. The shipped string is Delete this rooms?; corrected here, and the consequence names the room and the number of people affected rather than stopping at All invites sent will be deleted.

Sheet, or dropdown?

  • SheetMore than about six options, or options that need a second line, a crest, a search field or an Apply. Anything multi-select. Anything on a phone that a thumb has to reach.
  • DropdownThree to six short, single-line, mutually exclusive values that change one field in place — a season, a currency, a sort direction. Never multi-select, never with search, never with a footer.
  • NeverTwo visually identical controls opening two different surfaces. That is the fault on HF-02: Season opens a raw Material menu, Category opens a custom sheet, and nothing on screen tells you which is which.

Hall of Fame's Season control is genuinely a dropdown case — three short values, one field. Category is genuinely a sheet case — the award names run to Member of the Season and the surface is where the filter is explained. So the pair stays split, but both are rebuilt to the same language and the dropdown is registered properly.
