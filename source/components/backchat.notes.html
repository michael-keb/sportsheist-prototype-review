Backchat is tab 3 of 5 and the app's live-social surface. A fan lands here to walk into the club's always-on Fan Stadium, drop into a Locker Room that is running right now, or book a place in one that opens later.

What you're looking at

One hub, three ways into a room

The screen is deliberately short. A hero names the place, one photographic card opens the Fan Stadium, and a single Locker Rooms heading owns everything else — a container of rooms that are live now, and a container of rooms that are scheduled. Starting a room is the green + beside the heading; seeing the rest is the View all ↗ pill opposite it.

The live rooms sit in a horizontal carousel that snaps, so the second card deliberately peeks at the right edge — that peek is the only signal that the row scrolls, and it is intentional. Scheduled rooms stack vertically instead, because a date is something you read down a list rather than swipe through.

One thing worth flagging from the current live app: the word live is rendered in four different colours across this one feature — amber #DFAA11 on the locker-room card, a violet #542BE2 pill with a red #FF244C dot on the Fan Stadium card, a dark-violet #361F59 pill with a white dot inside the room, and red #E70A0A in the code — plus bronze #B08126 for the scheduled variant, and no marker at all on the live room screen itself. Here there is exactly one: the accent #9BE222. Scheduled rooms are distinguished by weight and fill, not by inventing a second hue.

Provenance

What is reused and what is new

Reused from the Figma file

  • Hero construction — 138px panel, 112deg field, acid peaks at 54% × 38%, prop in front of the peaks, uppercase title left. Same build as the Merits hero.
  • Section container, status pill, icon button, avatar, tab bar — lifted unchanged from merits.html, which sampled them from the Backchat and War Room dark-mode exports.
  • Token block — copied verbatim; no colour, radius or type value is invented.
  • Portraits — from the export set. taythaytu, capitan_7 and scout_mode get the same three faces they have on Merits, so a username reads as the same person across screens.
  • Content — Manager Auto Room, host taythaytu, the 1 / 2 counts and the open / locked room labels are the captured screen's own data. The Barcelona v Arsenal fixture and the April 19 date come from the captured Matches screen.

New in this proposal

  • Chevron hero field — a darker chevron texture (#000 at .06–.13) rather than the faint white triangles Merits uses at 2.4–3%.
  • Stadium card artwork — a CSS floodlit-pitch stand-in, because the photograph has not been exported. It is a placeholder, not a design.
  • One colour for "live" — the accent replaces four colours plus a bronze scheduled variant.
  • Green + circle — room creation becomes a 30px accent circle in the section header, replacing the full-width ＋ NEW LOCKER ROOM block. It is the only accent-filled control on the screen apart from the active tab.
  • Enter ↗ pill — replaces the circular chevron button that the live card clips a third of the way through.
  • Quiet UPCOMING pill — same pill, #13171E fill, muted label, no dot.
  • Reminder icon button — the third control is a bell (rooms you are reminded about). The live app's third control is a sort mark; sort is not exposed here.
  • Snap carousel — the live app clips the second card mid-word with no snap or peek treatment.
  • Two-line title box — titles clamp at two lines and reserve both lines inside the carousel, so footers align. The live app fixes card height at 125pt regardless of title length.
  • One colour for the footer figures — the live app renders three colours across the three values in that row.
  • Two radii, not nine — this screen is R16 and R12 plus pills. The captured screen renders nine distinct radii in one viewport.
  • 16px gutter — the captured screen uses 24pt; the code uses 16. The contract's 16px wins.
  • Live search — the magnifier opens a field that filters the carousel by room title or host.
  • Access filter sheet — open or locked rooms, with a live count and Clear all.
  • Reminder filter — the bell is a two-state toggle that narrows the carousel to rooms you have a reminder set on.

All three section-bar controls are live. Until this pass every one of them was an aria-hidden glyph on a button with no handler. Access is the only axis in the filter sheet because the open/locked badge is the only thing a live card actually carries beyond its counts — and the filter reads that badge back off the card rather than a second copy of the value. The bell filters to Manager Auto Room, which is the room Alerts says you set a reminder on, so the two screens agree. All three controls compose: a search plus a locked-room filter plus the bell can legitimately return nothing, and the rail then says so rather than going blank. The sheet closes on the scrim, on Esc and on its close button, returning focus to the glyph; closing without pressing Apply discards whatever was ticked.

Decisions

What we need from you

  1. Can we have the two photographs?

    Two images in the Figma frame are not in the export set: the knockout microphone that sits in front of the hero's acid peaks, and the stadium photograph behind the Fan Stadium card. The hero currently ships with the slot empty rather than with drawn clip-art, and the stadium is a CSS gradient stand-in. Both slots are marked in the markup and swap in without touching anything else.

  2. Should the Fan Stadium card name the room?

    As designed, the card carries only the two pills — no room name. That reads well over a photograph, but it does mean the club's flagship room is unlabelled, and the live app already gives it three different names: FAN STADIUM in the section header, BARCELONA'S STADIUM on the card and BARCELONA'S FAN STADIUM inside the room. We have put Barcelona’s Stadium in the card's accessible label only. If it should be visible, it needs a bottom-left slot on the card — and one canonical name.

  3. Two copy strings need fixing

    The shipped strings include Delete this rooms? (the delete-room dialog title) and TO BE REMINDED ROOMS (a filter option). Neither is on this screen, but both belong to this feature and both read as unfinished. We suggest Delete this room? and Rooms I'm reminded about — confirm and we will carry them through the sheets and dialogs.

  4. Is search / filter / reminder the right trio?

    The live app offers search, sort and filter, on both the live and the upcoming section. We have kept search, filter and reminder on the live container and given the upcoming container no controls at all, on the grounds that a short scheduled list sorts itself by time. If sort matters, it comes back as a fourth 38px circle — which is the practical limit at 375 wide.

  5. What do the two figures mean on a scheduled room?

    On a live card they are speakers in the room and people in the room. On a scheduled card there is nobody in the room yet, so we have read them as confirmed speakers and people booked in. The glyphs are identical in both places; if those are different quantities they may need different glyphs, or a label.

  6. Username or display name in the host row?

    The row renders host : club_staff — the account username, as the live app does. Elsewhere the same person appears as a surname in caps and as a Title Case name. We have assumed username is deliberate for a social surface; say if the host byline should use display names instead.
